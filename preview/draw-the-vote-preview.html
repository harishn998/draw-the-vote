India · 1951

Draw it so they cannot get it wrong.

India is about to hold the largest election ever attempted. A hundred and seventy-six million people may vote, and roughly eighty-five in every hundred cannot read a ballot paper.

No country had ever given everyone the vote at once. Every other democracy used reading as a reason to withhold it. India's answer was to change the ballot instead — so no party gets a name. Every party gets a picture.

You have to draw them. Six of them. And each new one must be impossible to confuse with every symbol already on the paper.
